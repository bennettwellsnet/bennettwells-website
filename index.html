About

Who I Am

I lead the infrastructure, cloud, and support teams that keep a consulting firm running. I care about systems that are secure by default, quietly reliable, and easy for people to use, and I've spent 25 years building them in places where compliance really matters.

My work centers on cloud migrations, hybrid networks, and a mixed Windows and Mac fleet of about 1,000 devices. On the security and compliance side, I'm the person who puts the controls into practice, working alongside security leads and auditors to support SOC 2, HIPAA, and FINRA programs.

I also manage a five-person infrastructure and end user computing team, handle vendor relationships, and work with business leaders to keep technology spending tied to what the firm actually needs.
